What ages do you accept?+

We welcome children from 3 months through 6 years across three programs: Infant Nest (3–18 months), Toddler Garden (18 months–3 years), and Casa dei Bambini (3–6 years). Each is specifically designed for that developmental stage.

What are the fees?+

Monthly tuition starts from 15,000 EGP per child and includes freshly prepared nutritious meals and snacks daily. A one-time non-refundable registration fee secures your child’s place. Contact us for the full fee schedule and sibling discount details.

What are your operating hours?+

We operate Sunday through Thursday, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Extended care until 6:00 PM is available. We follow the Egyptian Ministry of Education holiday calendar.

What is the Montessori Method?+

Developed by Dr. Maria Montessori, it’s a child-centered approach built on self-directed learning, hands-on materials, and respect for each child’s individual pace. Research consistently shows Montessori children develop stronger independence, creativity, and academic foundations.

Do you provide meals and transport?+

Yes — freshly prepared, nutritious meals and snacks are included in tuition fees daily. Transportation within Sheikh Zayed is available through our trusted partner service; details confirmed at enrollment.

How do you communicate with parents?+

Through a dedicated parent app with daily photos, activity logs, and observations. For infants, we share detailed feeding and sleep records. Teachers are available via WhatsApp and we hold formal parent-teacher meetings each term.

How do I enroll?+

Start with a free tour — book via this website or WhatsApp. If Haven feels right, we’ll guide you through enrollment. Documents needed: birth certificate, vaccination record, and parent ID. Early enrollment strongly recommended as places fill quickly.
